Layout

Media stage into masonry

Desktop uses an 83px rail and centers a 940px composer in the remaining canvas. The gallery enters before the first viewport ends. Mobile must replace the rail with a drawer and make the composer fluid instead of copying the source page's fixed-width clipping.

Guidelines

Preserve the hierarchy, not the branding

Do

  • Use real generated media as the main color source.
  • Keep glass dark, refractive, and transparent.
  • Reserve cyan, blue, and violet for focus energy.
  • Keep gallery gutters near 4px.

Don't

  • Do not add an opaque hero card.
  • Do not turn the whole UI purple.
  • Do not nest the composer in another panel.
  • Do not copy the source's mobile clipping.

Nebulens migration

Keep the existing beUI/shadcn component contracts, Nebulens logo, current video, and account flows. Adopt only the media-stage hierarchy, refractive material, rail proportions, and compact masonry rhythm.
