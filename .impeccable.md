## Design Context

### Users

Nebulens serves individual creators and small creative teams who need to discover visual ideas,
generate images from text or references, compare results, revisit prior work, and manage reusable
assets and generation quota in one focused workspace.

### Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and future-facing. The product should feel like a dependable creative instrument:
quiet application chrome, clear operational hierarchy, and expressive generated imagery rather
than decorative interface effects.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark-first, layered graphite surfaces with restrained Nebulens orange, cyan, and blue accents.
beUI Pro is the authoritative component and interaction source. Generated media supplies most of
the color. Morphing menus, tooltips, modals, and the prompt composer explain continuity without
turning the workspace into a glowing marketing page. Squarespace styling and generic AI-dashboard
card grids are explicit anti-references.

### Design Principles

1. Put the creation task or current assets first; keep marketing composition out of work routes.
2. Use source-owned beUI/shared semantic components for visible controls and preserve their
   keyboard, focus, disabled, pending, and reduced-motion behavior.
3. Keep product chrome neutral and compact so generated images remain visually dominant.
4. Use spacing, contrast, and progressive disclosure to maintain calm operational density.
5. Ship every visible action end to end and test empty, loading, error, unauthenticated, and
   responsive states rather than exposing unsupported decoration.
